{# ── The three views of the same marks ────────────────────────────────────────── Rendered by both student pages (dashboard + results) from this one file, and drawn by one factory — `window.sgVisualSummary`. Two implementations of "the same three charts" is how the two pages would start printing different distributions of the same marks, which is the defect this repository keeps paying for. Nothing here reads the host component's own rows: the points arrive as JSON built by the route (`_chart_points`) in *one shape for both pages*, already blanked for any mark the teacher has not released. That is also why the switch here is the `showScores` both pages already carry: a chart is a loud way to print the score the table masks, and a switch that hid the table while the axis printed every mark is a switch that does not work. Three states, and the two that are not charts used to be one grey sentence — "marks are hidden" and "nothing has been released yet" looked identical, so a reader whose marks were not out yet was told to press a button, and a reader who pressed it got empty axes. #}
